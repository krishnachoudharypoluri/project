# Store policies

## Returns
Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery with proof of purchase. Items must be in their original packaging. Final-sale items and gift cards cannot be returned. Contact support to receive a return authorization before shipping an item back.

## Shipping
In-stock orders usually leave the warehouse within two business days. Standard delivery typically takes three to five business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates may change during peak periods.

## Warranty
Warranty coverage varies by product and is listed in its specifications. Keep the order confirmation as proof of purchase and contact support for warranty service.
